Independent reference construction:
  Every +/-h and +/-2h point gets a fresh cold classify_cells() call with no
  warm D14 roots. The angular boundary is reconstructed from binary128
  quartic coefficients; real roots are isolated with the qf Sturm chain and
  safeguarded Newton-polished against the direct lens phi equation. F0 and
  sqrt(phi) are integrated directly; production analytic gradients and the
  production K-rule are not used. The radial integral uses composite
  Gauss-Legendre on the fold-mapped radial coordinate. A 5-point central FD
  is formed from fresh value integrals at h=rho*{1e-3,5e-4,2.5e-4}.
  The resolution ladder is recorded in summary.json. X uses a focused final
  160 x 32 radial order/subdivision grid; Y uses 128 x 16; both use 1024
  angular nodes. The resulting stability spread is an observed convergence
  diagnostic, not a formal interval enclosure. Radial cell cuts and fold
  mapping are regenerated by the same topology authority; the direct angular
  boundary/value path is independent of the production gradient evaluator.
