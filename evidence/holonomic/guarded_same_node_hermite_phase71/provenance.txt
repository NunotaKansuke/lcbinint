Comparison protocol
-------------------
RelTol: 1e-3 and 1e-4; atol=1e-16; GradientPolicy::None; HybridEmbedded;
nested_difference_safety=2. For each input/tolerance, baseline and shadow are
measured in alternating order. Full cold rebuilds D14/topology each epoch.
Full warm uses prepared L2 D14 roots with topology reuse disabled. Radial-only
builds each row's topology outside the timer and times flux_adaptive_integrate;
its AdaptiveWorkspace is reused within the trajectory. Each primary pass has
3608 uniform epochs per tolerance. The two untimed passes are pooled for the
primary quantiles; the instrumented run is component timing only.
