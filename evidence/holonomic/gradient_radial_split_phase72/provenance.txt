Both scripts use C++17, -O3, -march=native, -funroll-loops,
-ffp-contract=fast, -fno-math-errno, -DNDEBUG, -lquadmath and pin execution
to CPU 0. Four A/B arms: pure p-refinement (threshold 0), conditional split
after level 4, after level 5, and level-8 no-split estimator control. Each
corpus arm has 110 inputs * 2 tolerances * 2 repeats * 2 paths = 880 rows.
Each path/tolerance has 220 paired rows. Runs are rotated in order by case,
repeat, tolerance and path.

Cold path calls epoch_adaptive: fresh D14/topology plus adaptive value and
ValueFirst five-gradient integration. Warm path first makes one unmeasured
same-input epoch_adaptive_prepared call, then times prepared calls; D14 roots
are warm-started, while topology is reclassified (topology-plan reuse remains
off). The AdaptiveWorkspace is reused within each arm. Whole time includes
topology/D14 and integration; it does not include the unmeasured warm-up.
