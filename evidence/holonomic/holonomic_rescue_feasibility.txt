----------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANALYTICAL DERIVATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

t = tan(theta/2).  An inside image arc on |z| = R is bounded by two real roots
t_- < t_+ of the boundary quartic P(t;R).  Carry the symmetric pair

    m = (t_+ + t_-)/2 ,   v = ((t_+ - t_-)/2)^2 ,   t = m + sqrt(v) x , x in[-1,1]

(this is exactly the shipped (m,v) root-pair state -- root_pair.hpp).  Then

    (t - t_-)(t_+ - t) = v (1 - x^2)
    P(t)   = v (1 - x^2) S_2(t) ,   S_2(t) = P / ((t-t_-)(t_+-t)) > 0 on the arc
    Y := sqrt(P A B) = sqrt(v) . sqrt(1-x^2) . sqrt(S_2 A B)
      A(t) = 1 + t^2 ,   B(t;R) = (R-a)^2 + (R+a)^2 t^2

    J_{1/2}(R) = (2/rho) int_{t_-}^{t_+} P/(A Y) dt
               = (2/rho) . v . int_{-1}^{1} sqrt(1-x^2) . w(m + sqrt(v) x, R) dx
      with   w(t,R) = sqrt(S_2(t)) / ( A(t)^{3/2} sqrt(B(t,R)) )              (*)

  ==>  K(R) = (2/rho) int_{-1}^1 sqrt(1-x^2) w(m + sqrt(v) x, R) dx    (GC-2 wt)
  ==>  J_{1/2} = v . K   EXACT.

At the fold (v -> 0):

  K(R_*) = (pi/rho) . sqrt(C(m_*,R_*)) / ( A(m_*)^2 B(m_*,R_*) ) ,
  C = S_2 A B |_{m_*} = (1/2)|P''(m_*)| A(m_*) B(m_*,R_*)   > 0  and FINITE.

w(.,R) is analytic in t on a disc about t = m of radius r_conv = dist(m,
nearest complex singularity of w) = { complex roots of P (S_2 = 0),
t = +-i (A = 0), t = +-i|R-a|/(R+a) (B = 0) }.  Hence K is analytic in v:

    K(R) = sum_{k>=0} c_k(m,R) v^k ,  c_k = (2/rho) beta_{2k} w^{(2k)}(m,R)/(2k)!
    beta_{2k} = int_{-1}^1 sqrt(1-x^2) x^{2k} dx = pi/2, pi/8, pi/16, 5pi/128,...
    truncation error after N terms  ~  (v / r_conv^2)^{N+1}.

Numerically S_2 is the CLOSED-FORM deflation of P by (t-t_-)(t_+-t)
(period_reduction._deflated_quadratic) -- given (m,v) it needs NO root solve.

NUMERICAL PROBE RESULTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. K IS FINITE AND BIT-STABLE AS v -> 0     (scratchpad/rescue_out1.txt)

  Step off the exact D14 fold radius by dR in [1e-2 .. 1e-6]; compare the
  x-chart integral K_gc (48-node GC-2 on (*)) against the oracle K = J_half/v
  (J_half from the angular sqrt(phi) QAWSE, period_reduction).

    dR       v          J_half     K_oracle     |K_gc/K_oracle - 1|
    1e-2   ~1e-3       ~5e-2       ~30-130       1e-14 .. 1e-15
    1e-4   ~1e-5       ~5e-4       ~30-130       1e-13 .. 3e-13
    1e-6   ~1e-8       ~5e-6       ~30-130       3e-11 .. 4e-10 (J_half ~ eps)

  Holds for EVERY genuine fold across all 5 geometries.  The x-chart form is
  bit-stable exactly where the naive J_half/v ratio loses half its digits.
  => K is finite and well-conditioned as v -> 0.  Q1 PASS.

B. LOCAL v-SERIES SEED ACCURACY            (scratchpad/rescue_out1.txt)

    fold neighbourhood, genuine (small-v) folds, r_conv in [0.11, 0.61]:
      v           2-term (k<=1)   3-term (k<=2)   4-term (k<=3)
      ~1e-3        1e-5 .. 1e-6   4e-8 .. 1e-8    1e-10 .. 1e-11
      ~1e-4        1e-7 .. 1e-8   6e-11 .. 1e-11  <= 1e-12
      ~1e-5        1e-9 .. 1e-10  ~ machine       ~ machine

  Convergence ratio per order ~ v / r_conv^2, as predicted.  A 3-4 term
  v-series evaluates K(R_*) to 1e-11 or better for any v <= 1e-3.
  => the local series is a MACHINE-PRECISION SEED at the fold.  Q2 PASS.
  (Spurious "folds" with m_* ~ -8..-19 -- the theta->pi singular chart, NOT a
  tangency -- have r_conv ~ 10-24 and v ~ 1e1-1e2; the series converges slowly

C. CONDITIONING ACROSS GEOMETRIES          (scratchpad/rescue_out1.txt)

  fine but the arc leaves the (m,v) chart (kTransportTMax guard).  Basis (b).

D. TRANSPORT WINDOW -- NO PER-NODE ANGULAR RE-ANCHOR   (scratchpad/rescue_out_b.txt)

  Seed once at R_seed = R_* +- 3e-4 (local series -> (m,v,c_k)); march R
  outward with RK4 on root_pair_dR; evaluate K at every step two ways:

   T1  FROZEN v-SERIES : c_k frozen at the seed, only v(R) updated by the ODE.
       rel error grows ~ LINEARLY, ~1e-4 per 3e-4 of |dR|  (~ 0.3 |dR|).
       => NOT a transport.  The series is a SEED only: c_0(m,R) = K at v=0
          itself moves at O(1) rate in R.  Do not freeze it.

   T2  (m,v) ODE + x-CHART K : carry (m,v) by the shipped 2x2 IFT ODE;
       each step deflate S_2 in closed form from (m +- sqrt v) and apply an
       8-node GC-2 rule to (*).  NO angular quadrature, NO quartic root solve.
       rel error vs the angular oracle, ALL 5 geometries, whole marched span
       (|dR| up to 5-6e-2, v up to ~1e-2, through fold clusters):
             1e-14  ..  1e-11        (m-drift 1e-15 .. 1e-11)
       8-node and 16-node identical to ~1e-14  =>  8 nodes suffice.
       planet-wide: one seed carried an arc fold-to-fold across a full
       inter-fold cell (dR ~ 4.7e-2) at 1e-13 until it collapsed at the next
       fold.
  => Q5 PASS.  A single local-series seed + the (m,v) ODE + an 8-node analytic
     K rule transports J_{1/2} across an entire fold-neighbourhood / inter-fold
     cell with no re-anchoring.  This is the transport, not T1.

E. dK/dp FROM THE SAME RULE                (scratchpad/rescue_jac.py)

  Central-difference of the 8-node x-chart K vs central-difference of the

SIX-QUESTION CHECKLIST VERDICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. K STABILITY AS v -> 0 .................................... PASS
   x-chart K matches the oracle to 1e-13..1e-15 at every dR to 1e-6; finite
   closed form K(R_*) = (pi/rho) sqrt(C) / (A^2 B).  cond of the carrying
   (m,v) ODE stays 2-8 through the fold.

2. LOCAL-SERIES SEED ACCURACY ............................... PASS
   3-4 term v-series -> 1e-11 or better for v <= 1e-3; error ~ (v/r_conv^2)^N,
   r_conv in [0.11,0.61] for genuine folds.  Machine-precision seed, cheap,
   no period basis, no 1e12 conditioning.

3. CONDITIONING ACROSS REPRESENTATIVE GEOMETRIES ............ PASS
   (m,v) EO Jacobian cond 2-8, x-chart K quadrature O(1), across all folds in
   5 geometries incl. q = 1e-3.  No degradation as v -> 0.

4. IMPROVEMENT VS THE eta/psi BASIS (cond ~1e12) ............ PASS (decisive)
   5-9 orders better conditioned near a fold (2-8 vs 1e5-6e9, psi-closure
   resid 3.9e-3 near theta->pi).  CAVEAT: theta->pi degree drop is NOT
   addressed -- that is basis (b), not this spike.

5. TRANSPORT WINDOW WITHOUT PER-NODE RE-ANCHOR .............. PASS
   T2 = one local-series seed + (m,v) ODE + 8-node x-chart K holds 1e-14..1e-11
   across a whole fold-neighbourhood / inter-fold cell (|dR| to ~5e-2).  The
   frozen-series T1 is a seed only and must NOT be used as the transport.

6. REALISTIC PROSPECT OF BEATING THE DIRECT ANGULAR SWEEP ... QUALIFIED YES
   Per-node kernel ~13-16x lighter AND the quartic solve vanishes for fold
   arcs.  BUT this only covers fold-neighbourhood arcs.  Generic mid-cell arcs
   (v = O(1), no fold within seed/ODE reach) are untouched -- they still need
   the angular sweep or basis (b).  Epoch-level (from gauss_manin_feasibility:
   quartic solve 0.17 ms/14 % + angular sweep 0.23 ms/18 % of a 1.26 ms epoch)
   the rescue removes BOTH but only on the fold-adjacent fraction of the work.
   No C++ epoch measurement of that fraction here; from D14 event density
   ~25-40 % of the (solve+sweep) 0.40 ms is fold-adjacent and single-seed
   reachable  ->  ~0.10-0.16 ms  ->  epoch ~1.08-1.14x from (a) ALONE.
   The advisor's ~1.47x ceiling (both stages fully removed) needs (b) + the
   coupled (c) to also cover the generic cells.

transport; it is NOT by itself a generic-cell replacement for the angular
sweep.
