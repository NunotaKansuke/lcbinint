Phase B step 1 -- compensated (double-double) D14 solve  (2026-09-08)
====================================================================

Governing task: keep every bit of information D14 carries (real fold
events AND complex-root-derived panel boundaries), cut the COST of
solving it.  B0 verdict (d14_solve_cost_split.txt): the __float128 warm
polish `qpol` is 67.5% of radial_events median / 31.9% of the epoch
median / ~all of the radial_events p90 tail; coefficient construction
`d14c` is 4.2% and dead flat -> attack the SOLVE precision level.

Approach: run the Aberth-Ehrlich polish in double-double (unevaluated
sum hi+lo, ~106 bits, error-free transformations in hardware double +
one FMA per product) instead of libquadmath __float128.  D14 kappa ~1e9
sits well inside the double-double range.  Only a genuinely near-multiple
cluster (local kappa > ~1e14, detected by a per-root residual gate)
escalates that solve to __float128.

  src/lcbinint/magnification/holonomic/dd_real.hpp   -- DD type + EFT
  re_detail::solve_d14()  (radial_events.hpp)        -- multi-tier solve
  HOLO_D14_LEGACY_SOLVE=1                            -- A/B escape hatch
                                                       (exact legacy path)

solve_d14 tiers:
  balanced `double` presearch (decision-21 monomial balancing, s = |c_d/c_0|^(1/d))
   -> tier 0  double-double Aberth polish, 25 sweeps, tol 1e-26, dd seed
              gate: worst __float128 residual <= 1e-13
   -> tier 1  __float128 warm polish seeded by the dd roots (near-multiple
              cluster past 106 bits); gate 1e-12
   -> tier 2  cold __float128 Aberth, 400 iter, tol 1e-22 (seed non-finite
              OR tier-1 gate failed OR Vieta completeness check failed)
  tier 3 = legacy path (HOLO_D14_LEGACY_SOLVE=1): __float128 warm polish
           straight off the double presearch, no dd stage.

Completeness check (B0 verdict: "float128 solving was never itself a
completeness proof -- inclusion verification is a separate need"):
Newton's first identity  sum(roots) == -c[1]/c[0].  A dropped or doubled
basin -- the way an under-resolved Aberth actually fails -- shifts the
deg-14 power sum far outside rounding.  Absolute tol 1e-6*(1+|want|) on
O(1..10) roots is ~1e5x the honest round-off; on failure -> cold re-solve.
0/115 cases tripped it on the bench.

Conjugate symmetrization: D14 has real coefficients, so its non-real
roots are exact conjugate pairs -- a pair's two real parts are equal.
Finite Aberth splits them ~1e-11 rel in __float128, ~1e-8 rel in
double-double; radial_events dedups the complex-Re soft-boundary list at
a fixed 1e-9 gap, so the wider dd split would post one pair as TWO soft
boundaries.  Every near-conjugate pair (clearly complex, conjugate
distance < 1e-6 rel) is snapped onto its common real part and mean
imaginary magnitude.  No-op for the __float128 paths.
