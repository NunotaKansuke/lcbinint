ANALYTICAL DERIVATION
============================================================================

1. THE COUPLED PER-ARC STATE
----------------------------

For one image arc on |z| = R, bounded by real roots t_- < t_+ of the
boundary quartic P(t;R), carry the symmetric pair

    m = (t_+ + t_-)/2 ,   v = ((t_+ - t_-)/2)^2 ,   t = m + sqrt(v) x

(the shipped root_pair.hpp state).  Its radial ODE is the 2x2 implicit-
function solve  (dm/dR, dv/dR) = -[d(res)/d(m,v)]^-1 . d(res)/dR  of the two
symmetric-function residuals of P (root_pair.root_pair_dR).  This block is
AUTONOMOUS: it needs nothing but itself and the quartic coefficients.

Two scalar companions ride on top of (m,v):

  (i)  K(R) = (2/rho) int_{-1}^{1} sqrt(1-x^2) w(m+sqrt(v) x, R) dx        (a)
       w = sqrt(S_2) / (A^{3/2} sqrt(B)) ,  S_2 = P/((t-t_-)(t_+-t)) the
       closed-form deflation.  Then  (2/rho) Phi_arc = J_{1/2} = v . K
       EXACTLY, and K is finite and smooth as v -> 0 (spike a).  K is a
       functional of (m,v) and R only -- it does not depend on the jet.

  (ii) y_k = s^k Phi_arc^(k)(R_c)/k! , k = 0..d, d <= 6, s = max(H, r_a/4)
       -- the spike-(b) flux-priority jet.  Reconstruct Phi_arc(R) by a
       degree-d Taylor packet.  y is a functional of Phi_arc's local germ
       at R_c only -- it does not depend on K.

So the coupled state  X = [ (m,v) | K | y_0..d ]  has a BLOCK-TRIANGULAR
sensitivity structure:

       d/dR  (m,v)  =  f(m,v; R)                 (autonomous)
       K(R)         =  K[(m,v)(R); R]            (algebraic in the state)
       y(R)         =  transported germ of Phi_arc  (independent of K)

There is no K <-> y coupling and no feedback into (m,v).  A perturbation of
the seed propagates through at most one off-diagonal block, so the coupled
conditioning is  max( cond(m,v) , cond(K|(m,v)) , cond(y) )  -- it cannot be
worse than the worst stand-alone block.  (Verified: P3.)

2. THE THREE TRANSPORT REGIMES AND THE CHEAP ROUTER
--------------------------------------------------

F0 (image area) needs only Delta_theta_arc = atan2(2 sqrt(v), 1+m^2-v).2,
which the autonomous (m,v) block supplies in every regime -- no Gauss-Manin,
no jet.  F_half needs (2/rho) Phi_arc, and here the regime matters:

  regime 1  (fold in reach).  Phi_arc has a genuine sqrt branch point in R
     where an image band is born/dies (a physical_real event, v -> 0).  Use
     the spike-(a) rule  (2/rho) Phi_arc = v . K  with the 8-node x-chart K:
     v carries the vanishing, K stays O(1).  Seed = the algebraic (m,v) pair
     at R_c.  NO QAWSE evaluation.

  regime 2  (generic, incl. the theta->pi degree drop).  Phi_arc is analytic
     in a disc that overreaches the cell (spike b: apparent singularity at
     chart_p4, decay_rho 3..1300).  Use the flux-priority jet: one cheap
     d+3 QAWSE seed near R_c, reconstruct by the Taylor packet.

  regime 3  (fail closed).  arc_chart raises (a genuine theta=0 AND theta=pi
     straddle -- the arc cannot be made bounded in either chart), OR no
     (m,v) real pair exists at R_c, OR the K-rule returns non-finite.  Fall
     back to the incumbent angular sqrt(phi) sweep with an explicit status.
     Never a silent approximation.

THE ROUTER IS CHEAP AND USES NO QAWSE FOR REGIME 1:

  dist_fold  = | v(R_c) / (dv/dR)(R_c) |     (v ~ (R - R*) near a fold, so
               this is the algebraic distance to the band-birth radius)
  regime 1  if  dist_fold < 2 H          (fold within two half-widths)
  regime 2  if  not regime 1  AND  decay_rho >= 6   (decay_rho from the
               d+3 jet-seed Chebyshev coefficient tail; the jet is only
               taken when the local germ is provably analytic well past
               the cell)
  regime 3  if  arc_chart raises  /  no pair  /  K non-finite  /  (regime 2
               was provisional but decay_rho < 6 -> demote to regime 1, the
               universal rule; only a hard failure reaches 3)

3. WHY THE K-RULE IS THE UNIVERSAL FALLBACK, NOT THE JET
-------------------------------------------------------

The identity (2/rho) Phi_arc = v.K holds for ANY bounded arc, fold or not:
S_2 = P/((t-t_-)(t_+-t)) is a polynomial (exact deflation) that is positive
and smooth on the arc whenever the arc exists, and A,B > 0 there.  Nothing in
the x-chart quadrature of K degrades away from a fold.  So regime 1's
machinery -- (m,v) RK4 + 8-node x-chart K -- is a correct transport of
Phi_arc on EVERY 'arcs' cell, including through the theta->pi degree drop
(the reflected chart that arc_chart picks for a near-pi arc keeps the arc
bounded; K is computed in that chart).

The jet (regime 2) is NOT more general -- it FAILS when a fold or a real
Phi_arc branch point sits within its analyticity radius (decay_rho -> 1..3,
reconstruction error 1e-2..1e-4; see the note-bracket column of P1).  Its
value is purely SPEED: on a generic cell it is a 7-term Horner evaluation
(P6: 66x the direct kernel) versus the x-chart K's 8-node quadrature (15x).
Both remove the quartic solve and the angular sweep; the jet removes more
constant factor on the cells where it is safe.

Consequence for the ceiling:  regime 1 alone -- routing EVERY cell to the
K-rule -- already removes both the quartic warm solve (14% of the epoch,
checkpoint sec.22.1) and the 64-pt angular sqrt(phi) sweep (18%), because
the x-chart K is 15x cheaper than the direct kernel, far below the ~3x that
would make it the bottleneck.  So the 1/(1-0.32) = 1.47x epoch ceiling is
reachable from regime 1 alone; regime 2 only widens the margin below it.

4. SEED COST ACCOUNTING
-----------------------

The incumbent re-anchors the period at EVERY radial quadrature node (spike b
premise): ~n_r QAWSE-class evaluations per cell (n_r = 64 panel nodes in
classify_cells, or >= 21 in the reference flux).  The coupled state seeds
ONCE per cell:

  regime 1 :  0 QAWSE.  The seed is the algebraic (m,v) pair at R_c (a
              deflated quartic evaluation) plus the x-chart K, which is an
              8-node Gauss-Chebyshev-2 rule on a rational integrand -- not a
              sqrt(phi) angular sweep.
  regime 2 :  d+3 = 9 QAWSE near R_c for the jet germ (this also yields
              decay_rho, so the router costs nothing extra).
  regime 3 :  falls back to the incumbent per-node sweep, by construction.

P3.  COUPLED-STATE CONDITIONING (BLOCK-TRIANGULAR CHECK)

  Perturb the (m,v) seed by 1e-6 relative; measure the induced relative
  drift of (m,v) and of K at the cell edge; compare with the stand-alone
  spike-(b) cond(y) on the same cell.

  kappa(m,v)  seed->edge, fold cells (v small)    ~ 1e0 .. 5e5
              seed->edge, generic cells           ~ 1e0 .. 8e0
  kappa K     seed->edge, all cells               1e-4 .. 0.16
  cond(y) b   (unchanged by the coupling)         3e1 .. 1e8

  The large kappa(m,v) on fold cells is the expected relative amplification
  of a quantity going to zero (absolute drift is at the seed level); K
  DE-amplifies (kappa < 1); cond(y) is exactly the spike-(b) number -- the
  coupling introduces no new off-diagonal growth.  Coupled conditioning
  = max of the three blocks, as derivation sec.1 predicts.

OVERALL VERDICT:  GO.  The coupled (m,v)+K+flux-priority per-arc state is a
single block-triangular transport that, seeded once per radial cell, carries
both flux integrands across the whole cell -- fold cells, generic cells, and
the theta->pi degree drop alike -- to <= 3e-8 relative on F_half and <= 2e-8
on F0, with 100% cell coverage and 0 fail-closed over 6 representative
geometries.  The cheap algebraic router (dist_fold + decay_rho, no QAWSE for
the fold path) selects the K-rule or the jet correctly on every cell.  The
3-solver benchmark shows the (m,v) stage alone at ~1.16x epoch (== the
shipped HOLO_MV_TRANSPORT) and the full holonomic stage removing both the
quartic solve and the angular sweep, so the ~1.47x epoch ceiling is
reachable at 100% coverage.  Proceed to the C++ 3-solver benchmark of the
unified engine.

Caveats / scope:
  * Feasibility on the pure-Python oracle.  The C++ port must build the
    (m,v)+K+jet companion transport, the algebraic dist_fold router, and
    the fail-closed status path, then measure real epoch timings under the
    decision-20 gate.  This spike does NOT touch solver code.
  * Wide cells (w >~ 0.08) show K-rule F_half error up to 3e-8 from RK4
    (m,v) accumulation over the node spacing; the fixed substep here is
    min(3e-4, H/6).  A finer fixed substep or a sub-cell split makes it
    integration-limited-only, not fundamental.
  * The jet is least accurate at cell edges (P4 caustic-xing 3.3e-6); the
    per-cell planner should either shrink the jet's trusted sub-interval or
    hand edge nodes to the K-rule.  The K-rule has no such edge degradation.
  * decay_rho from a 9-node local Chebyshev coefficient tail is noisy at the
    low end (reports ~1 whenever the tail does not decay cleanly).  It is a
    conservative gate (>= 6 to allow the jet), so noise costs a little
    jet-coverage, never accuracy -- those cells fall to the universal
    K-rule.
  * regime 3 (genuine theta=0/theta=pi straddle, arc_chart raises) did not
    occur for any dominant arc in these 6 geometries.  Sub-dominant arcs
    that straddle theta=pi would fail closed to the angular sweep with an
    explicit status (never a silent approximation).
  * P6 absolute microseconds are under load ~15; op-count and ratio are the
    signal (same caveat as spikes a and b).
