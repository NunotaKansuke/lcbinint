--------------------------------------------------------------------------
What changed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. src/lcbinint/magnification/holonomic/fp_env.hpp  (NEW)

   ScopedFlushDenormals -- RAII guard that sets MXCSR FTZ (bit 15) + DAZ
   (bit 6) on x86 and restores the caller's MXCSR on scope exit.
   Installed as the first statement of holonomic::flux_jacobian and
   holonomic::epoch_jacobian (epoch_jacobian.hpp).

   Rationale: a near-converged Aberth iteration on the boundary quartic
   drives the intermediate |p|, |p'| and the root-gap |z_i - z_j| (near a
   tangency) into gradual underflow. On x86 each denormal SSE operand
   costs a ~50-100x microcode assist. This dominated the warm-started root
   solve and inflated the cold solve too. Every quantity the solver
   reports is >= ~1e-3 in magnitude and every retained polynomial
   coefficient is >> 2.2e-308, so flushing sub-normal INTERMEDIATE values
   to zero is numerically inert here. The __float128 D14 path is software
   (libquadmath) and is unaffected by MXCSR.

2. src/lcbinint/magnification/holonomic/radius_terms.hpp

   real_root_thetas_warm(pc, QuarticWarm&) -- warm-starts the degree-4
   Aberth solve at radial node k from node k-1's converged roots (the
   Gauss-Chebyshev nodes are monotone in R within a cell; the quartic
   roots move smoothly with R away from a tangency).

   Adopt-warm certificate (all three required):
     * final Aberth max-correction step <= 1e-7 (free: returned via the
       new final_step out-param of aberth()); the quartic roots are only
       seeds for the downstream 6-iteration polish_endpoint Newton, so
       ~1e-9 root accuracy is already past what is used;
     * real-root count unchanged from the seed;
     * same polynomial degree.
   Any miss -> exact cold 40-iteration solve, bit-identical to
   real_root_thetas(). After 3 consecutive misses the warm path is
   disabled for the rest of the cell (moving tangency). QuarticWarm is
   reset at each cell boundary and at a chart (p4~0) radius.

3. Dead-code cleanup (user is code-quality focused):
     * removed aberth_roots_plausible<R>() from poly_roots.hpp -- the
       Vieta/Newton completeness certificate used by warm-start v1-v3,
       replaced by the free final_step proxy;
     * removed the unused iters_used out-param of aberth().
