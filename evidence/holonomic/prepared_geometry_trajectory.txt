----------------------------------------------------------------------------
FINDINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. L2 (Phase B2 all-root D14 warm-start on the rolling PreparedEpochGeometry)
   is SAFE and DEFAULT-ON.
     * exact parity with the cold path -- median / p90 |dmu|/|mu| = 0.
       The max 3.92e-07 (grad 3.05e-02) is the Aberth iteration count
       differing between a warm and a cold seed, well inside solve_d14's own
       convergence tolerance -- NOT a topology or completeness difference,
       because classify_cells still runs every epoch and remains the
       authority.
     * FALSE REUSE 0, status downgrades 0 -- fail-closed holds.
     * 1.08x steady-state median speedup; p90 / p95 / p99 all NON-REGRESSING
       (in fact improved: p99 3.16 vs 3.35 ms).  The M7 / decision-20 gate
       was already MET by B1 (1.71x epoch median); this is additive.
     * warm seed consumed on 3348 / 3348 non-cold epochs (100%): the previous
       epoch's 14 roots are always finite and correctly sized.

2. L1 (verbatim cached-cell-plan reuse, quartic-only re-screen) is NOT
   default-safe and stays gated OFF (allow_topology_reuse = false).
     * only 14 / 3456 epochs even reached an L1 reuse -- the per-cell quartic
       topology re-screen rejects the candidate 1026 / 1040 times it is
       attempted (drift <= l1_drift).
     * of those 14, 5 were FALSE REUSE: |dmu|/|mu| up to 2.47e-2, ||dgrad||
       up to 3.49, on near-caustic epochs.  The re-screen is built entirely
       from the boundary quartic (quartic_topology, quartic_disc_sign) and is
       structurally blind to D14's complex-root-derived panel boundaries and
       to the fact that a reused cell plan places quadrature panels at the
       ANCHOR geometry, not the drifted one -- near a caustic that panel-
       placement error alone is percent-level.
     * this is the same wall Phase A hit ("physical_complex soft boundaries
       not reconstructible from the quartic").  A cheaper-than-D14 topology /
       panel certificate is designated future research (checkpoint sec.0
       policy point 3), so L1 remains opt-in for experiments only.
     * L1 also buys almost nothing on top of L2 here (1.09x vs 1.08x).

3. The prepared-geometry plumbing works: PreparedEpochGeometry (Layer-1
   immutable payload: all 14 D14 roots + CellPlan + r_max + conditioning
   margins + status + provenance), a caller-owned rolling cache threaded
   through epoch_jacobian_prepared exactly like the QuarticWarm workspace
   precedent, no new public API.

CONCLUSION: Phase B2 lands as the L2 warm-seeded D14 solve on the Phase E
prepared-geometry cache -- exact, fail-closed, 1.08x on trajectory workloads,
tails non-regressing.  Phase E's L1 verbatim topology reuse is implemented
and benched but held behind an off-by-default flag pending the deferred
cheaper-than-D14 certificate.
