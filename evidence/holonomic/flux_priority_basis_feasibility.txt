============================================================================
ANALYTICAL DERIVATION
============================================================================

1. THE theta->pi DEGREE DROP, PRECISELY
---------------------------------------

t = tan(theta/2).  Q(t;R) = P(t;R) . A(t) . B(t;R),  degree 8 in t, with
  A = 1 + t^2 ,  B = (R-a)^2 + (R+a)^2 t^2 ,  P = boundary quartic,
  q8(R) = p4(R) . (R+a)^2 ,   p4(R) = Re( rho^2 R^2 (R+a)^2 - |c_T2|^2 ).

A `chart_p4` radial event is p4(R*) = 0 : a boundary root of P crosses
theta = pi  <=>  t -> infinity  <=>  deg P : 4->3  <=>  deg Q : 8->7.
It is a REPRESENTATION event -- no image band is born or dies (that is a
`physical_real` / disc-sign event).  The period LATTICE is unchanged; only the
chart that trivialises the t = infinity behaviour degenerates.

The eta/psi residue-free basis subtracts the t = infinity residue of
eta_3 = t^3 dt/sqrt(Q) using the asymptotics
  sqrt(Q) ~ sqrt(q8) t^4 ( 1 + b1/t + b2/t^2 + b3/t^3 + ... ),
  b1 = -q7/(2 q8),
  b2 = -q6/(2 q8) + 3 q7^2/(8 q8^2),
  b3 = -q5/(2 q8) + 3 q7 q6/(4 q8^2) - 5 q7^3/(16 q8^3).
psi = ( eta0, eta1, eta2, eta4 - b1 eta3, eta5 - b2 eta3, eta6 - b3 eta3 ).

As q8 -> 0 with q7,q6,q5 generically O(1):  b1 ~ q8^-1, b2 ~ q8^-2,
b3 ~ q8^-3, and their R-derivatives b_j' ~ q8'/q8^(j+1).  The connection
C_psi = (W_R + W C_eta) W^+ inherits these -- poles of order up to 4 in
(R - R*).  Hence cond(C_psi) ~ q8^-k -> 1e9..1e12, and the residue-free
closure condition  h3' = h3 + b1 h4 + b2 h5 + b3 h6 == 0  degrades to
O(1e-3) in floating point.  (All reproduced numerically -- P1 below.)

2. THE SINGULARITY IS APPARENT FOR THE PHYSICAL FLUX
---------------------------------------------------

The half-flux contribution of an arc bounded away from theta = pi,
  Phi_arc(R) = (rho R / 2) int_arc sqrt(phi) dtheta
             = c(R)^T . Pi(R)   (reduced form; c = (h0,h1,h2,h4,h5,h6)),
has integrand and endpoints (roots of P) that are smooth in R as long as
THIS arc's endpoints stay away from theta = pi and do not collide.  A
`chart_p4` event is a DIFFERENT root of P (another arc, or a non-physical
crossing) passing through theta = pi: q8 hits zero but Phi_arc(R) for this
arc is real-analytic through R = R*.

Formally Phi_arc(R) satisfies a scalar Picard-Fuchs ODE
  sum_{j=0}^{r} a_j(R) Phi^(j) = g(R) ,   r <= 6
(the reduction P/A == H + d_t(S Y/A), deg_t H <= 6, gives 7 monomial periods
I_0..I_6; I_3 carries the infinity-residue, leaving physical rank r <= 6).
The a_j(R) are polynomials in the q-coefficients; the leading a_r(R) carries a
factor vanishing at R* (that IS the 1/q8 pole in companion form).  But R* is
an APPARENT singularity: local exponents are non-negative integers
{0,1,...,r-1, r+n}, n >= 1, with NO logarithm -- every Frobenius solution is
holomorphic.  Phi_arc and a full basis of the physical period solutions
extend holomorphically across R*; only the psi chart degenerates.

3. THE FLUX-PRIORITY BASIS
-------------------------

Since Phi_arc(R) is analytic across the cell with radius r_a (= decay_rho . H,
H = cell half width), transport the SCALED DERIVATIVE / companion basis of the
scalar ODE, seeded from ONE positive physical integral at the cell midpoint
R_c:

    y_k = s^k . Phi_arc^(k)(R_c) / k! ,   k = 0 .. d ,   d <= 6 ,
    scale  s = max( H , r_a / 4 )    (i.e. scale to the ANALYTICITY radius,
                                      never below the cell half-width).

Transport   dy/dR = M(R) y   with M the (rescaled) companion matrix of the
scalar ODE (bottom row -a_j/a_d, sub-diagonal s-ratios).  Reconstruct the
period / flux anywhere in the cell by
    Phi_arc(R) = sum_k y_k ( (R - R_c)/s )^k   (+ tail, negligible at k = d).

Conditioning  cond(y) = max_k |y_k| / min_k |y_k| :

  * naive  s = H :  Phi analytic in radius r_a means |Phi^(k)/k!| ~
    Phi_max r_a^-k, so |y_k| ~ Phi_max (H/r_a)^k = Phi_max decay_rho^-k and
    cond ~ decay_rho^d.  Generic cells decay_rho in [3,15] -> cond 1e2..1e6
    (target met); narrow event-adjacent slivers decay_rho in [110,1300] ->
    cond 1e7..5e10 (target missed by 1-4 orders).
  * rescaled  s = r_a/4 :  |y_k| ~ Phi_max (s/r_a)^k = Phi_max 4^-k, bounded
    and mildly DECREASING, so cond -> O(4^d) ~ 1e1..1e3 regardless of
    decay_rho.  MEASURED (P6): every degree-drop cell, slivers included,
    drops to cond(y_0..5) = 30..900.

The narrow-cell blow-up under s = H is a SCALING ARTEFACT: those cells are
precisely the ones where Phi_arc is far more analytic than the cell is wide
(decay_rho up to 1272), so the higher derivatives are genuinely negligible on
the H scale and the max/min ratio explodes even though the physical content
is a 2-3 term packet.  Scaling s to r_a rebalances the sequence.  Not an
obstruction.

4. WHY A CONSTANT RE-BASING OF psi CANNOT DO THIS
------------------------------------------------

A constant similarity G maps C_psi -> G C_psi G^-1; it cannot create or
cancel an R-dependent pole.  To cancel b_j'(R) ~ q8^-(j+1) in C_psi, G would
itself have to carry unbounded (b_j-like) entries -- i.e. G = G(R), which is
exactly a NEW basis (the flux-priority one), not a re-basing.  Worse (P3): at
a chart_p4 cell edge deg Q < 8 and the psi connection C_psi is NOT DEFINED at
all (the residue-free chart requires the t = infinity residue that q8 = 0
removes) -- there is nothing there for a constant G to re-base.  The fix must
be structural: build the basis on Phi_arc, which is analytic through q8 = 0,
not on the eta/psi periods, whose chart degenerates there.

OVERALL VERDICT:  FEASIBLE (unconditional).  The flux-priority basis -- the
scaled derivative/companion basis of the scalar Phi_arc Picard-Fuchs ODE,
seeded from one positive physical integral per cell, scale s = max(H, r_a/4)
-- achieves cell-wide conditioning of O(1e1..1e3) THROUGH the theta->pi
degree drop (generic cells and event-adjacent slivers alike), versus
O(1e9..1e12) for eta/psi -- and where eta/psi is not merely ill-conditioned

The apparent-singularity structure is what makes this possible and it holds
across all 7 representative geometries.  (b) REVIVES.

Caveats / scope:
  * This is the CONDITIONING result the advisor asked for.  It does NOT yet
    build the companion-matrix transport in C++ or measure speed -- that is
    part of (c).
  * Wide cells (decay_rho ~ 3-6, "order for 1e-8 = None") want either > 6
    Taylor terms or a 2-packet split; the per-cell planner already knows the
    cell width and can choose.
  * The seed is ONE positive integral of Phi_arc and its low derivatives at
    R_c (not the cancellation-prone c^T Pi) -- a single deflated QAWSE or a
    short local series, NOT a per-node re-anchor.
  * Fail-closed is natural: if the Chebyshev/derivative probe of Phi_arc at
    the seed shows decay_rho < ~2 (analyticity radius under the cell), split
    the cell or fall back to the angular sweep.
