decision
--------
  - chart_p4 exact branch and legacy parity: adopted.
  - fixed-degree GM algebra and scalar precision measurements: adopted as
    isolated research kernel improvements.
  - true GM whole epoch as V2 replacement: not adopted.
  - V3 9-point K-rule/Chebyshev packet: retained as a separately named
    comparison path; it is not counted as true GM transport here.
  - next required direction: direct scalar flux-priority companion basis or
    another regularized GM representation that remains defined across the
    chart_p4 degree drop and supplies analytic parameter derivatives.
